How URL submission works
Instant backup check
Pasted URLs are checked automatically. If a gallery is already publicly backed up with an archive, you'll get direct links to the gallery and its archives right away — no need to submit it.
Batch friendly
Add links one by one, paste a list, or drag & drop a .txt file. Duplicates are detected automatically, and you can prune already-backed-up links in one click.
Moderator review
Submitted URLs enter the review queue where moderators verify and archive them. Adding an optional reason helps reviewers prioritize your request.
